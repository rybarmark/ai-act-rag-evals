### en-fact-004  (factual, en)

**Q:** Do companies that use AI have to train their staff on it?

**Reference:** Not training as such. Article 4 requires providers and deployers of AI systems to take measures to ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level of AI literacy of their staff and others operating or using AI systems on their behalf, taking into account their technical knowledge, experience, education and training and the context of use. The Act does not prescribe which measures.

> **Art. 4 (en):** Providers and deployers of AI systems shall take measures to ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level of AI literacy of their staff and other persons dealing with the operation and use of AI systems on their behalf, taking into account their technical knowledge, experience, education and training and the context the AI systems are to be used in, and considering the persons or groups of persons on whom the AI systems are to be used.

*Note:* An earlier reference said 'Yes', which overstates Article 4: training is a factor to consider, not a required measure.
